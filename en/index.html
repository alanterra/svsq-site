Siempre Viva San Quintín Field Station

The Siempre Viva San Quintín Field Station will be the premier field research and education station in Baja California.

Mission

We promote the study of biodiversity, ecology, conservation, and other scientific programs across the Baja California peninsula, especially in the San Quintín region. We will host field education programs at all levels, from secondary to postgraduate and professional training.

What we’re building (now)

  • Site preparation and core infrastructure for research stays
  • Design of academic and training programs
  • An initial activity calendar and facility-use guidelines
